# Offline CMMC assessment: source import and draft score calculator

This is an **engineering preview**, not a complete or independently validated assessment methodology.

## Authoritative sources, versions, and their limits

- CMMC Level 2 is based on 110 NIST SP 800-171 Revision 2 requirements for the currently published Phase I program.
- NIST SP 800-171A (June 2018) provides the procedures corresponding to the Rev 2 baseline. NIST withdrew the 2018 publication in 2024 in favor of Revision 3; NIST explicitly states that the **2018 PDF is normative** relative to its supplemental CSV/XLSX. A 2018 objective import cannot silently be treated as Rev 3.
- Scoring deductions follow 32 CFR 170.24(c)(2); two controls (3.5.3 and 3.13.11) permit narrow 3-point partial-effectiveness deductions.
- A software tool does not authorize government submission, C3PAO accreditation, or certification. These actions remain with the authorized organization and government systems.

Sources:
- https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/171/a/final
- https://dodcio.defense.gov/cmmc/About/
- https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-170.24

## 1. Import the full local NIST source CSV

Download the official supplemental CSV in a **connected, approved staging environment** from:

https://csrc.nist.gov/files/pubs/sp/800/171/a/final/docs/sp800-171a-assessment-procedures.csv

Inspect the file, establish and record an expected SHA-256 from the acquired original, and transfer the approved CSV to the offline workstation. Then:

```sh
./freecompliance cmmc objectives-import --csv sp800-171a-assessment-procedures.csv --sha256 <EXPECTED_LOWERCASE_SHA256> --scope "Approved CUI enclave" --out objectives.json
mkdir -p evidence
./freecompliance cmmc objectives-assess --input objectives.json --evidence-dir evidence --json objective-report.json
```

The importer requires recognizable requirement and objective columns and rejects malformed records, cross-control objective IDs, duplicate objective IDs, any missing requirement among the 110, or a checksum mismatch. It preserves the imported objective statements. It does **not** independently verify that the CSV matches the authoritative PDF. If the official CSV format is not recognized, the importer fails closed rather than guessing. The expected digest must be independently obtained; calculating the digest from an untrusted substitute does not establish source authenticity.

A source import and 110 populated requirements are **structural completeness**, not proof that the assessor performed every necessary test. Potential assessment methods are assessor-selected, not automatically executed.

## 2. Create a draft scoring worksheet

```sh
./freecompliance cmmc score-template --scope "Approved CUI enclave" --out score-input.json
```

All 110 findings start **UNASSESSED**. A human reviewer must enter every finding and reviewer/rationale, confirm that an up-to-date SSP exists, and resolve exceptions before running:

```sh
./freecompliance cmmc score-draft --input score-reviewed.json --json draft-score.json
```

This command applies the enumerated 1/3/5-point categories in 32 CFR 170.24(c)(2). The exceptions are: `3.5.3` with `PARTIAL` and `partial_case: REMOTE_AND_PRIVILEGED_MFA`, and `3.13.11` with `PARTIAL` and `partial_case: ENCRYPTED_NOT_FIPS_VALIDATED`. It rejects partial credit for any other requirement, missing reviewers, missing SSP confirmation, missing controls, and unsupported baselines.

**A draft score is NOT a validated SPRS score.** The tool cannot independently establish an assessor's identity, control effectiveness, method adequacy, scope applicability, SSP currency, or government acceptance. The output is labeled `DRAFT_USER_SUPPLIED_NOT_VALIDATED` and never submitted automatically. Do not represent this as a certification.

## 3. Remaining delivery gates

- Cross-check *every imported objective and statement* against the 2018 normative PDF with independent expert signoff and explicit counts. The importer alone does not close this requirement.
- Read-only, reviewed Windows/Linux/identity/network adapters with positive and negative tests; an evidence hash alone cannot prove effective operation.
- Independantly audit 110 scoring weight mappings and the two limited partial exception paths against official published examples and assessor feedback.
- Produce actual OSCAL Assessment Results outputs validated against the pinned official NIST OSCAL schema with round-trip interoperability tests. There is no general-purpose 'OSCAL certification' granted by NIST simply by exporting JSON.
- Government SPRS submission remains an authorized human/approved government interface action; accredited third-party assessments and government endorsement cannot be generated by software.

These features must be completed and verified before changing the public product claim from *CMMC readiness workbench* to *complete assessment solution*.
