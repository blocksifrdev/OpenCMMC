# Enterprise Proof Challenge | BlockSiFr FreeCompliance

## Your compliance tool says the control passed. Does the evidence actually prove it?

Modern security teams automate collection and monitoring. But a passing control indicator does not necessarily establish that every relevant technical pathway is protected. FreeCompliance adds a free, open-source verification layer that tests **precise claims** against admitted evidence, searches bounded models for counterexamples and refuses unsupported assurances.

### One workflow. One critical claim. Thirty days.

Bring a consequential action, such as an AI agent deploying code, an administrator changing a privileged rule, or an automated system modifying infrastructure. We select a narrow technical assurance statement, collect authorized read-only evidence, run bounded theorem checks, and ask an independent reviewer to adjudicate the result.

**You receive:** a versioned claim and scope definition; evidence provenance and timestamps; supported, counterexample or unknown results; a documented remediation/evidence-acquisition plan; and a transparent time-on-task and false-assurance scorecard.

**You retain:** the free Apache-2.0 software, JSON outputs, and source-bound evidence records. No mandatory SaaS subscription and no forced BlockSiFr Control conversion.

**Limitations:** This does not issue a SOC 2 report, CMMC certification, FedRAMP authorization, legal opinion, or guarantee of operational safety. A modeled proof remains conditional on explicitly scoped assumptions, source coverage and independent validation.

**Optional 30-day assisted proof engagement:** Proposed fixed fee **$25,000** for one workflow and one primary evidence system, subject to a signed statement of work. Customer-operated use of the open-source utility remains free.

**Next step:** Nominate one CISO/compliance sponsor, one engineering owner, and one reviewer. Bring one action you cannot afford to execute incorrectly.
